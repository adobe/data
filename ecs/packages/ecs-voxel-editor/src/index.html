Help

Click or drag to select faces; hold Shift for a line, Ctrl for a rectangle, double-click for connected faces. + extrudes with the picked material or model, − deletes; P switches to paint mode, where + paints and − takes paint off. Ctrl Z undoes. Tab enters the instance under the pointer to edit its model, Shift Tab leaves; Delete removes it.

Middle drag or Alt drag orbits, with Shift pans, with Ctrl zooms; two fingers orbit too, and pinching zooms. W A S D turn the view; numpad 1 3 7 for views, N for the starting view, Home to frame.

This browser has no WebGPU, so the scene can't be drawn.
